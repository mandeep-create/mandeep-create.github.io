GUU 2.0 · Singularity Theory

◉ The Singularity

All five scales of the Grand Unified Universe orbit one point. Planck matter circles closest, the Meta shell drifts farthest out, and everything slowly spirals inward. Nothing that crosses the event horizon r₊ comes back as it was.

⚖ Conservation

Matter is never created or destroyed. Each particle that crosses the horizon is held inside it and adds its mass m to the singularity, so the horizon grows. Hawking radiation can only return matter the singularity is holding, and each emission takes its mass back out. At every moment In Field + In Horizon = Σ Matter. Polarity poles are created in ± pairs, so net charge Q stays zero. Energy lost to viscosity in the accretion disk leaves as its glow. Gravity on the field follows the shell theorem: matter that falls in was already pulling on everything outside it, so swallowing it grows the horizon without changing the pull on distant orbits.

Knowledge is not conserved. Through decoherence, cold matter far from the singularity slowly drifts back toward the background Known/Total ratio.

🎬 Cinematic 3D & the Spacetime Grid

In cinematic mode the camera orbits the singularity in true 3D perspective. Light from the far side of the disk is lensed over the top of the shadow, and the side of the disk rushing toward you shines brighter (relativistic beaming). The spacetime grid is a rubber-sheet picture of curvature: it sinks into a well that deepens as the singularity gains mass, and each polarity pole dents (+) or lifts (−) it. Switch cinematic mode off for a flat top-down view.

⟲ Spin & the Ergosphere

A spinning singularity drags space with it. Higher spin shrinks the event horizon and widens the ergosphere (the dashed violet boundary), forcing nearby matter to co-rotate.

✧ Hawking Radiation & the Information Paradox

Matter that falls in is lost to the Unknown. Hawking radiation returns it as Known matter bursting from the horizon. Lighter singularities radiate harder (TH ∝ 1/M), so a singularity that grows heavy radiates less. Info Back tracks how much of what fell in has returned.

✸ The Big Bounce & Relativistic Jets

When the horizon holds the Bounce Threshold share of all matter, the singularity turns critical and rebounds. Everything it held is released at once as Known matter in a shockwave. While matter pours in quickly, twin jets fire along the spin axis; their length follows the accretion rate.

🌈 Spectrum

Spectral Energy colors matter by kinetic energy, from slow red to fast violet. Doppler Shift colors matter moving toward the camera blue and matter moving away red. Invert Colors renders the field as a photographic negative, like a spectrograph plate.

📐 Polygarity & 🔗 Multitarity

Polarity poles orbit inside each shell, pulling (+) or pushing (−) matter into local fields. Multitarity links join particles across scales; they are born, glow and dissolve continuously, and they bend around the singularity under gravitational lensing.
