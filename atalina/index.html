PROCUREUR FUSTREDIS · THE MOVIE

A 16:66 film in 4D made from the whole book. All 77 books of the Latin Vulgate play as trailers in nine acts, each with its own scene, colour and key verse. The book's 1,797 pages stream past in a codex column, beside live counts of pages, chapters, verses, words and letters. Cryptographs decode from 16 ancient scripts and are read aloud in Latin by electro voices, each in its own octave and timbre, and Hebrew and Greek lines appear with their numbers. An electro score runs underneath, with trailer impacts at every cut.

The VCR is part of the film. Shuttle it from ten times backwards to ten times forwards and the tape, the light, the text and the sound all respond.
