The singularity

General relativity predicts that every path inside ends here, at a moment of infinite density. Nobody knows what really happens. The leading proposals are unproven:

  • a quantum bounce into a new expanding region
  • a Planck star: matter stops at the Planck density and rebounds
  • a fuzzball: string theory replaces the interior with a tangle of strings
