    THE RUBRIC

    How to write sound by hand in Chirographia Magna.

    The gestures of the Quill

    TAPOne note of the chosen value, at the pitch under the quill.
    SWEEP →A held note, as long as the sweep.
    CURVEA melody: one note per step of the grid, following your line; repeated pitches join.
    DRAW ↓ or ↑A chord: thirds stacked in the key between the top and the bottom of your stroke.
    SCRIBBLERub back and forth over notes to erase them (or use the Erasure).
    PEN · PENCILWrite words, marks, fingerings or sketches by hand anywhere on the page: the Pen writes in ink (five colours), the Pencil in graphite. They do not sound; the Erasure rubs them out, and they are kept, exported and printed with the folio. With a stylus, resting your palm is ignored.
    PEN PRESSUREWith a pen, how hard you press sets how loud the notes are; otherwise the Touch (pp … ff) does.

    The Triad

    NODE · LexThe law of the key: pitches snap to the scale and time snaps to the grid.
    ANTINODE · SpeculumEvery note is answered by its mirror image, reflected about the middle line, on the counter-stave (concept).
    NO-NODE · AetherThe line exactly as drawn: free glides between pitches, microtones, unquantised time (meend, portamento).

    The Chronotape Deck

    The whole folio is printed to a master tape every time you write. The deck plays that tape like a real machine: varispeed moves pitch and tempo together, ⇄ plays it backwards, wow and flutter wobble the capstan, and the tape formulation (or VCR mode) sets bandwidth, hiss and saturation. ● records a cassette of everything you hear; while recording, taps on the folio land under the tape head (overdub). Click the ruler above the staves to move the tape, drag it to scrub, shift-drag it to set a loop.

    The Consistory of Checks

    Instrument ranges, one voice per stave, parallel fifths and octaves (a style rule, not a law), leaps, the low interval limit, density, notes outside the key, and the master tape's measured peak and loudness. Click a check to find it on the page.

    The Observatory

    VU needles, peak columns with hold and clip lamps, momentary, short-term and integrated loudness (live figures are approximate; the tape figure is exact), phase correlation, the oscilloscope, the spectrum with the strongest pitch named, the spectrogram, the goniometer, and the Harmonic Lens, which names the chord under the tape head. Wheel or pinch on the scope, spectrum and spectrogram to zoom; double-click to reset.
