Grand Unified Universe Theory

📐 Polygarity Principle

Multiple simultaneous poles/attractors at different scales create competing gravitational, electromagnetic, and dimensional fields. The known universe observes only one pole while infinite poles exist in the unknown universe.

🔗 Multitarity Principle

Many-to-many dimensional interconnections allow information flow between observed and unobserved scales. A single event at one scale ripples across infinite dimensional hierarchies.

🌌 Deep's Paradox

The universe exhibits infinite depth that cannot be fully comprehended by finite minds. Each explanation reveals deeper layers. The formula itself becomes paradoxical at infinite depths.

  • n: Dimensional index (2D to 11D shown)
