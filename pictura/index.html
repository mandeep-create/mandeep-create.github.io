La Musa the sound companionCONCEPT
Recital play the painting

Each medium has its own sound under the hand. The colour in hand sets the key around the circle of fifths, its lightness the register; higher on the panel is higher in pitch, and a faster hand plays more notes. Panning moves the sound, zooming out opens the cathedral, and each stratum changes the chord. The church modes and the instruments are historical; this mapping is our invention.

Vista how the atelier looks

Vista changes the atelier around the panel. The painting itself is always shown true, so colours are judged fairly; the conservator's lenses on the Banco tab are for looking at the painting.
