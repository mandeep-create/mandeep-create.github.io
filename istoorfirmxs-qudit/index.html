Every sound is synthesized live in your browser. No recordings or samples are used.
  • EstablishedMetal set: every bar, bell, plate and drum head is modal synthesis, a bank of decaying partials at the object's real mode ratios (a free bar rings at 1 : 2.76 : 5.40 : 8.93; tuned xylophone and marimba bars at 1 : 3 : 6 and 1 : 4 : 10). Cymbals and hats add a metallic oscillator bank and a noise wash; drums add a falling pitch sweep.
  • EstablishedStrings set: plucked strings use the Karplus–Strong digital waveguide: a delay line one period long with a loss filter, the standard physical model of a vibrating string.
  • EstablishedBowed strings (violin, cello, any zone in pizzicato or bow) use a two-part waveguide with a friction curve, after J. O. Smith's bowed-string model.
  • EstablishedWhere you pluck changes the tone: plucking near the end is bright, plucking at the middle removes the even harmonics. Harmonic mode plays real natural harmonics at ½, ⅓, ¼ and ⅕ of the string.
  • EstablishedSympathetic resonance: strings tuned to a unison, octave or fifth of the note you play start ringing on their own, as on a sitar.
  • ApproximationThe sitar and veena buzz (jawari) is modelled as a one-sided bridge barrier. Each instrument's wooden body is two resonant filters, not a full body model.
  • EstablishedThe sitar zone has 7 playing strings tuned Ma, Sa, Pa, kharaj Sa and the chikari Pa, Sa, Sa', and Zone VI holds 13 taraf strings tuned to the raga. Taraf strings are never plucked on a real sitar; they ring in sympathy, as they do here.
  • ConceptDensity zoom: zooming in adds strings between the strings, tuned to the microtones in between, so the screen stays full of strings at the same spacing. Real instruments cannot do this.
  • Sound designAmps, pedals, the orchestra layer and the cinematic layer are effects designed for this instrument, in the spirit of a plugged-in rig.
  • EstablishedWave markers show the physics of a standing wave: nodes stay still, antinodes move most, no-node strings are at rest. String motion is slowed down so you can see it.
