OI.IO · Single Black Hole Singularity Mechanism

◉ The Mechanism

One spinning black hole sits at the heart of the Grand Unified Universe. Five scales of matter, from Planck to Meta, orbit it in nested shells and slowly spiral in through a blazing accretion disk. Nothing that crosses the event horizon r₊ comes back as it was.

⚖ Conservation

Matter is never created or destroyed. Each particle that crosses the horizon is held inside it and adds its mass m to the singularity, so the horizon grows. Hawking radiation can only return matter the singularity holds, and each emission takes that mass back out: In Field + In Horizon = Σ Matter at every instant. Polarity poles are born in ± pairs, so net charge Q stays zero. Gravity on the field follows the shell theorem, so swallowing matter grows the horizon without changing the pull on distant orbits. Energy lost to viscosity leaves as the disk's light.

⟲ Spin & Frame Dragging

The spin slider runs from clockwise ⟳ (left) through zero to anticlockwise ⟲ (right), seen from above the north pole; its size is the spin speed. The disk, the orbits, the poles and every field line turn with it. Faster spin shrinks the horizon and widens the ergosphere.

⚡ Gravity, Magnetic & Toroidal Fields

Gravity lines (neon lime) pour into the singularity, twisted into spirals by frame dragging. Magnetic loops (electric cyan) are anchored to the spinning horizon; the spin winds them up and the Field Twist climbs until the Big Bounce snaps them back in a reconnection flash. Open magnetic lines coil around each jet and collimate it. Toroidal lines (neon magenta) wind around nested tori that wrap the disk and blaze while matter pours in. Pulses of light race along every line.

✦ Quasar Jets

While the singularity feeds it fires two razor-thin relativistic beams along its spin axis. Knots of energy race outward along each beam and radiation sparks off its sides. Their reach follows the accretion rate and the Jet Power control.

🌌 Lensing & Gravitational Waves

Starlight passing the black hole is bent into an Einstein ring around its shadow, and light from the far side of the disk is lifted over the top. Every Big Bounce sends gravitational-wave ripples racing across the spacetime grid.

✧ Hawking Radiation & the Big Bounce

Hawking radiation returns swallowed matter as Known matter; lighter singularities radiate harder (TH ∝ 1/M). When the horizon holds the Bounce Threshold share of all matter it turns critical and rebounds, throwing everything it held back into its shells.
